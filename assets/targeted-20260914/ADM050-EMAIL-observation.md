# ADM050-EMAIL-001 transport observation

A pre-deployment controlled KYC confirmation email dated 11 September 2026 was opened through Gmail. Its confirmation link reached the canonical deployed HTTPS verification route without a TLS interstitial, then redirected the existing signed-in member session to its dashboard.

This is only transport evidence. A dashboard redirect does not prove confirmation token acceptance. Fresh confirmation generation and resulting verification state were not tested. Token-bearing URLs, mailbox addresses and raw Gmail screenshots are intentionally excluded.
